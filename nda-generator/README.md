# NDA Generator - Professional Non-Disclosure Agreement Creator

A comprehensive web-based platform for creating legally sound Non-Disclosure Agreements tailored for freelancers, startups, and businesses hiring contractors.

## 🚀 Features

### Core Functionality
- **Three NDA Types**: Mutual, One-way, and Multi-party agreements
- **Step-by-step Wizard**: Intuitive 4-step process
- **Customizable Terms**: Flexible confidentiality periods, governing law, and purpose
- **Template Saving**: Save and reuse common agreement configurations
- **Document Download**: Export agreements as TXT or DOC files
- **Legal Disclaimers**: Clear guidance on legal limitations

### User Experience
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual progress bar for form completion
- **Real-time Preview**: See your NDA before finalizing
- **Smart Validation**: Required field validation with helpful feedback
- **Modern Interface**: Clean, professional design

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

- **HTML5**: Semantic structure and accessibility
- **CSS3**: Modern styling with CSS Grid and Flexbox
- **Vanilla JavaScript**: No frameworks, lightweight and fast
- **Local Storage**: Template persistence
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first approach

## 🎯 NDA Types Supported

### 1. Mutual NDA
- **Use Case**: Partnerships, joint ventures, collaborations
- **Description**: Both parties share confidential information with mutual obligations
- **Features**: Two-way protection, equal obligations

### 2. One-way NDA
- **Use Case**: Employee/contractor agreements, client relationships
- **Description**: Single party discloses confidential information
- **Features**: Simple structure, one-sided protection

### 3. Multi-party NDA
- **Use Case**: Complex business relationships, joint ventures
- **Description**: Three or more parties sharing confidential information
- **Features**: Scalable party addition, complex agreement structure

## 📝 Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Select Agreement Type
- Choose from Mutual, One-way, or Multi-party NDA
- Visual cards with descriptions and use cases

### Step 2: Party Information
- **Mutual**: Party A and Party B details
- **One-way**: Disclosing and Receiving party details
- **Multi-party**: Add multiple parties dynamically

### Step 3: Terms & Conditions
- **Confidentiality Period**: 1, 3, 5, 10 years, or indefinite
- **Governing Law**: US Federal, State, UK, Canada options
- **Purpose**: Detailed description of information sharing
- **Exceptions**: Custom permitted disclosures

### Step 4: Review & Download
- Real-time document preview
- Download options (TXT, DOC)
- Save as template for future use
